# StockCommand Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 17, 2024**

---

## 1. Introduction

StockCommand ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our service.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Email address
- Name (optional)
- Password hash (if using email/password auth)
- Google ID (if using Google OAuth)

### 2.2 Usage Data
- Trading signals viewed
- Competition participation and rankings
- Feature usage patterns
- Session data

### 2.3 Broker Connection Data
- OAuth tokens (encrypted)
- Broker account identifiers
- Connection timestamps

**We do NOT collect:**
- Your broker account number
- Your brokerage balance or holdings (only fetched on-demand, never stored)
- Your trade history at your broker (only fetched on-demand)
- Your bank account information
- Your Social Security Number

### 2.4 Technical Data
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Cookies and similar technologies

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---------|-----------|
| Provide the Service | Account info, broker tokens |
| Process payments | Email, Stripe customer ID |
| Improve the Service | Anonymized usage data |
| Customer support | Account info, usage data |
| Competition rankings | Username, performance metrics |
| Security | IP address, session data |

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

| Third Party | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Stripe | Payment processing | Email, subscription status |
| Cloudflare | Infrastructure | Technical data (encrypted) |
| Your connected broker(s) | Trade execution | OAuth tokens, order requests |
| Google (if using OAuth) | Authentication | Google ID |

**We do NOT:**
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with advertisers
- Share your broker credentials with anyone

---

## 5. Broker Data

When you connect a broker:

1. **OAuth Flow**: You authorize access on your broker's website
2. **Token Storage**: We store encrypted OAuth tokens to maintain connection
3. **On-Demand Fetching**: Portfolio/position data is fetched only when you request it
4. **No Persistent Storage**: Your holdings and balances are not stored in our database
5. **Revocation**: You can disconnect at any time via Settings or your broker's app

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement:
- Encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Encrypted credential storage
- Cloudflare DDoS protection
- Session-based authentication
- Regular security reviews

---

## 7. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Account data | Until account deletion |
| Broker tokens | Until disconnection |
| Usage analytics | 12 months (anonymized) |
| Chat history | 90 days |
| Competition data | Indefinite (public rankings) |

## 8. Your Rights

You may:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Correct**: Update your account information
- **Delete**: Request account deletion
- **Disconnect**: Remove broker connections at any time
- **Export**: Request data export (JSON format)

Contact us at [privacy@stockcommand.com] to exercise these rights.

You can disable non-essential cookies in browser settings.
